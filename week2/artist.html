CAI XUKUN

KUN, whose real name is Cai Xukun, was born on August 2, 1998, in Wenzhou, Zhejiang, China. He is a Chinese singer, songwriter, music producer, and actor. In 2012, he appeared on the television show Up! Young and began his journey in the entertainment industry. In 2015, he participated in the Chinese reality show Super Idol. In 2018, he gained popularity after joining the talent show Idol Producer, where he won first place and became the center of the boy group NINE PERCENT. After the group disbanded in 2019, KUN continued his solo career. His music combines different styles, including R&B, hip-hop, EDM, and pop. He is known for his musical creativity, unique style, and performances.
